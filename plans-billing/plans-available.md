# Plans Available

You can access your plan from the homepage at any time. All subscriptions come with a hassle-free 15 day trial.

The Notes List provides pricing models in a few models:

* **Individual** (1 Seat Subscription Model)
  * Discounted Educational Edition Available
* **Institution** (5 Seats Subscription Model with ability to purchase additional seats.)
  * Discounted Educational License Available
* **Per Show Pass** (One Time purchase with unlimited seats for one production.&#x20;
* **Viewer** (Any user can view The Notes List without a subscription.)

To register for a plan please visit: [thenoteslist.com/billing](https://thenoteslist.com/billing)

{% hint style="info" %}
NOTE: The Individual and Institutional subscription for Student/Educational editions require verification of a current .edu email address.
{% endhint %}

### Adjusting your plan

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Go to billing settings

Navigate to **Homepage → Billing → "Manage Billing" OR "View Plans"**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Select a new plan

Select the plan you are wanting to subscribe to or buy a single show pass.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Confirm payment

Follow payment steps and begin your subscription with a hassle-free 15 day trial.&#x20;
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To register for a plan please visit: [thenoteslist.com/billing](https://thenoteslist.com/billing)

### Cancelling

To cancel your subscription, go to **Homepage → Billing.**
