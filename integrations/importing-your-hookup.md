# Importing Your Hookup

This page will dive into integrations via one of the three main categories:

* Lighting Hookup Import



Lighting Hookup importing can be crucial when addressing your lighting note taking processes. Importing the hookup to The Notes List will allow for the typical fixture information you'd find in industry standard lighting paperwork to be provided directly in the note export.&#x20;

Such information as (but not limited to):

* Position
* Unit Number
* Fixture Type
* Wattage
