# Production Settings

Let's take a look at the settings tab and how we can customize our productions to fit our needs.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### General Production Settings

After you've created the production the settings tab, featured in the modules on the left side of the screen, will provide many options of customization.&#x20;

Under **Production Settings** you will be able to find the ability to:

* Adjust the production name/abbreviation
* Share the production with collaborators
* Add a thumbnail image for the production
* Adjust your modules
